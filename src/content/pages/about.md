Field Notes is a small, independent website. It's run by one person, updated
irregularly, and built to stay out of its own way.

There's no editorial calendar and no growth target. Posts go up when there's
something worth saying — a working note, a short essay, an observation that
survived a week of second-guessing.

## What you'll find here

Mostly writing about software, the web, and the practice of making small
things well. Occasionally something unrelated sneaks in, because that's how
personal sites are supposed to work.

## Get in touch

There's no contact form here on purpose. If you've read something and want
to argue with it, that's a good sign — hold onto the thought.
